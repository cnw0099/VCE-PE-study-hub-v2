VCE PE Study Hub: Base
🎯 Inverted U: arousal and performance

Blue dashes: complex or fine skill (golf) peaks at lower arousal. Orange dashes: simple or gross skill (AFL tackle) peaks higher.

Too low: lack of motivation, slow reactions, slow decisions. Too high: anxiety, jerky movements, poor decisions.

VCAA answers: state the theory, then compare two sports using “whereas”.

🏋️ Newton, momentum and impulse
  • 1st (inertia): stays at rest or constant velocity unless a net force acts. More mass means more inertia.
  • 2nd: F = m × a. Same mass, more force = more acceleration.
  • 3rd: equal and opposite reaction (racquet and ball).
  • Momentum = m × v
  • Impulse = F × t = m × Δv
  • Momentum breaker: increase time to reduce force (soft hands). Maker: more force and a follow-through.
  • Torque = force × moment arm

AerobicCHO, fats, proteinSlowHigh
  • All 3 always contribute. Use IDRY: Intensity, Duration, Rate, Yield, and link to the stimulus.
  • Fatigue: PC or glycogen depletion; H+ and Pi build-up; heat (blood diverted to skin).
  • Recovery: active (5 to 10 min low intensity) after glycolytic or aerobic work; passive after ATP-PC (98% PC back in 3 min).
🏋️ Levers and stability
  • Third class: force in the middle (A-F-R). Resistance arm is longer than force arm, so MA is below 1: built for speed (bicep curl).
  • MA = force arm ÷ resistance arm.
  • Stability: centre of gravity, base of support, line of gravity.
❤️ Acute and chronic responses
  • Acute: tidal volume, respiratory rate and ventilation (= TV × RR) rise; stroke volume and heart rate rise; Q = HR × SV.
  • Chronic: needs about 3 sessions a week for 6 to 8 weeks. Always link adaptation, effect, then performance.
  • After aerobic training Q is unchanged at rest and submax but increases at max.
📝 Exam format and technique
  • 120 marks: 15 multiple choice, 105 short answer. 15 min reading, 2 hours writing.
  • ABCPE: Annotate, Bracket the task word, Count the marks, Plan, then write.
  • In reading time, find the easy marks and count the short answer questions.
  • Stuck? Move on and come back. Answer every multiple choice.
  • Frantic? Breathe and sip water for one minute.
🧩 Answer templates
  • Critique: strength + reason, weakness + reason, improvement + why, then a conclusive statement.
  • Evaluate A vs B: same criteria for both, evidence for each, final line “A is better because…”.
  • 10-marker: plan 5 points; each is claim, reason, link to the stimulus (sport specific). About 12 minutes, then conclude.
  • Every question: read it twice, write concisely, name the sport.
